# Recruiter and HR Replies

Use this for `/ai-job-search:reply` and recruiter, HR, hiring-manager, LinkedIn DM, WhatsApp, email, salary questions, notice questions, work authorization, interview scheduling, assessment requests, rejection replies, and follow-ups.

## Rules

- Draft concise, natural, professional replies.
- Keep tone confident, not needy.
- Salary, current salary, notice, work authorization, sponsorship, and availability must come from current chat, source files, Job Search Profile, or explicit input.
- If current salary or another material fact is missing, ask instead of inventing.
- Do not send automatically.

## Output

```markdown
## What this message needs

## Copy-ready version

## Notes / missing info
```

If the message is urgent, say so. If no reply is needed, say that directly.

## Copy-Ready Snippets

Missing salary facts:
"Thanks for reaching out. I can share my expected range once I understand the role scope, location, and overall package. Could you share the salary band for this position?"

Authorization uncertainty:
"Thanks for checking. My work authorization depends on the role location and employer sponsorship process. Could you confirm whether the company is open to sponsoring or supporting work authorization for this position?"

Notice period unknown:
"I can confirm availability after reviewing the offer timeline and start-date expectations. Could you share the expected start date?"

Interview scheduling:
"Thanks for the update. I am available at [time option 1] or [time option 2]. Please let me know which works best for the team."
