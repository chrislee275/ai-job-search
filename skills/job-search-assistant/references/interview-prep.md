# Interview Prep

Use this for `/ai-job-search:interview`, interview prep, recruiter screens, HR screens, technical interviews, assessments, take-home tests, salary questions, work authorization questions, career gap framing, why role/company, and mock interviews.

## Workflow

1. Use the JD if available.
2. If no JD is provided, ask once whether the user has one.
3. If no JD is available, prepare from the user's target role/profile.
4. Cover technical, behavioral, salary, work authorization/sponsorship, career gap if relevant, why role, why company, and role-story answers.
5. Keep answers simple, natural, and truthful.

## Mock Interview

- Ask one question at a time.
- Wait for the user answer.
- Score the answer.
- Give a more natural/improved version.
- Continue only after the user is ready.

## Company Research

Research company facts when preparing "why company", recent product/company context, recruiter-screen motivation, or questions to ask the interviewer. Verify current facts when browsing is available. If not available, label assumptions and avoid specific claims.

## Answer Coaching Example

Weak answer:
"I think I can do this role because I am interested in data and I learn fast."

Feedback:
- Too generic.
- No role-specific proof.
- "I think" weakens confidence.

Improved answer:
"This role fits the reporting and stakeholder work I have already done. In my previous projects, I gathered requirements, cleaned data, and turned findings into summaries that helped the team decide next steps. I am still building deeper SQL experience, but I can contribute on analysis, dashboards, and business communication from day one."
