# Example: International Sponsorship Reply

Prompt:

```text
/ai-job-search:reply

The recruiter asked whether I require sponsorship. I am not sure how to answer.
```

Expected behavior:
- Use the user's confirmed work authorization facts if available.
- If missing, ask for location, current status, and sponsorship needs.
- Provide a recruiter-friendly reply with placeholders if needed.
- Do not invent visa status or eligibility.

Copy-ready pattern:

```text
Thanks for checking. My work authorization depends on the role location and employer sponsorship process. Could you confirm whether the company is open to sponsoring or supporting work authorization for this position?
```
