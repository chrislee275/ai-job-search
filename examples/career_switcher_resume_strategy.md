# Example: Career Switcher Resume Strategy

Prompt:

```text
/ai-job-search:resume

I am moving from operations coordination into business analyst roles. I have Excel reporting, stakeholder coordination, and process documentation experience, but only basic SQL from a course. How should I position my resume?
```

Expected behavior:
- Highlight transferable evidence.
- Mark SQL as exposure or developing skill, not professional proficiency.
- Suggest truthful bullets and keywords.
- Avoid pretending the user already worked as a business analyst if they did not.

Example wording:

```text
Built transferable experience in reporting, stakeholder coordination, and process improvement, now applying those skills to business analyst roles.
```
